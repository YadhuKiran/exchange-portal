# TEAM ROLE ALLOCATION

## Project Title
**Global Student Mobility & Exchange Portal**

---

## 1. Project Objective
The primary objective of the Global Student Mobility & Exchange Portal is to provide a unified, highly secure, and scalable web platform that digitizes and streamlines the international academic exchange process. The system is designed to seamlessly bridge the gap between university administrations and students by facilitating cross-border program discovery, digital application processing, secure document verification (including visas and transcripts), and enrollment management across a global network of partner institutions.

## 5. Project Management Structure
The project adhered to an agile-inspired management structure. The Team Lead served as the central hub for all technical decisions, while maintaining continuous communication with the QA Engineer for iterative feedback and bug resolution. The Documentation Coordinator operated in parallel, actively translating completed technical milestones into academic reports. This tri-layered approach (Development, Validation, Documentation) ensured steady progress and alignment with university submission deadlines.
